Industrial floor scrubbing is decided by what the soil is and how far it has worked into the slab, not by how large the floor is. A 2,000 m² machine shop with oil film bonded into concrete is a heavier job than a 20,000 m² storage hall carrying dust. That distinction sets the machine class.
