Two things decide the choice in retail: whether the machine’s footprint and passability match the store’s aisles, and whether it can operate during trading hours. Store format determines which of the two binds. In convenience stores, pharmacies and fashion retail, fixtures are dense and aisles are narrow, so passability is the first constraint.
